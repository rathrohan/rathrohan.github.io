Safety Warnings
Read all warnings before beginning installation
Safety first! Before we touch anything, let's go over the critical warnings. These protect you, your property, and ensure a compliant installation. Please don't skip this section!

⚠Commercial installation must be performed by a qualified licensed electrician.

🔐 Sensors
Sensor Installation
QSR and PQSR sensor installation procedures
Your LILY (GSR+) supports two sensor types: the QSR (magnetic mount) and the PQSR (inline connector). Choose the one included with your kit and follow its specific steps.
● QSR Sensor
Step 1: Loosen the cross-shaped round head screw and remove the cover on the back of the strip light. (Fig. 5)
Step 2: The front magnet of the sensor attaches to the sensing hole on the back of the lamp. (Fig. 6)
Step 3: Connect the 12V power to the sensor cable, turn ON the fixture, and verify sensor operation. (Fig. 7)
● PQSR Sensor
Step 1: Use a screwdriver to open the cover and remove the 12V power cable from the fixture. (Fig. 8)
Step 2: Insert the removed 12V connector into the PQSR sensor connector. (Fig. 9)
Step 3: Insert the PQSR sensor, straighten the wire neatly, power ON, and confirm full operation. (Fig. 10)
⚡ Connection Types
QSR uses a magnetic mount — no screws needed for attachment. PQSR uses an inline 12V connector that daisy-chains from the power supply. Both require 12V power input and should be tested after connection.
